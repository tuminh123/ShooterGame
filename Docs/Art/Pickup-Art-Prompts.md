# Pickup sprite generation

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, transparent background enabled. The originals were copied into the project; Unity imports them as single sprites with Point filtering, no texture compression and a 64-pixel maximum texture size.

- Gold: `Assets/_Project/Graphic/Pickups/GoldPile.png`
- Experience (active): `Assets/_Project/Graphic/Pickups/ExperienceGem.png`
- Original crystal cluster (unused alternative): `Assets/_Project/Graphic/Pickups/ExperienceCrystal.png`

## gold

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: tiny pickup sprite for an existing Unity top-down medieval roguelike pixel-art game, Vampire Survivors era. Create ONE small low mound of stacked gold coins, isolated centered on a genuinely transparent canvas. Coarse crisp pixel art visually equivalent to a 24x24 pixel sprite enlarged with nearest-neighbor, extremely simple readable silhouette, dark brown 1-pixel outline, muted ochre gold, amber shadows, 2-3 pale yellow glint pixels, limited 6-color palette matching dusty beige stone-ground tiles and small medieval pixel characters. Slight top-down three-quarter view. Coin pile occupies roughly central 70 percent of canvas, no text, no letters, no numbers, no ground, no background, no soft shadow, no smooth gradients, no antialiasing, no glow.

## experience gem (current)

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production-ready experience pickup sprite for a top-down medieval pixel-art roguelike, matching the user's screenshot: ONE SMALL BLUE GEM, an upright compact faceted ruby-shaped blue jewel with a pointed bottom and clipped shoulders. Just ONE gem, not a crystal cluster. Visually a 12 by 16 pixel icon enlarged in exact nearest-neighbor blocks: dark navy outline, cobalt-blue right facet, medium turquoise center, pale cyan upper-left glint, 5 or 6 solid colors. Small silhouette like a classic retro game experience gem. Centered and occupying 80% of a square canvas with minimal transparent padding. Genuinely transparent background and hard opaque pixel edges. Flat limited palette only: NO glow, no halo, no gradients, no shadow, no texture, no blur, no antialiasing, no text, no ground, no extra objects. It should be immediately readable as an individual blue collectible gemstone at a very small in-game size.
